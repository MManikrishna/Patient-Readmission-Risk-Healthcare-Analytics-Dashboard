Patient Readmission Analysis
An end-to-end data analytics project designed to help hospital operations and care management teams identify patients at high risk of 30-day readmission, analyze key risk drivers, and build actionable business intelligence dashboards for early clinical intervention.

Business Problem & Context
Hospital readmissions within 30 days of discharge are a major cost driver in healthcare and a primary indicator of care quality. High readmission rates lead to significant financial penalties under programs like the Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP) and point to gaps in discharge planning.

Strategic Goals
Operational Efficiency: Enable care coordinators to allocate post-discharge resources (follow-up calls, home visits) to high-risk patients.

Financial Impact: Reduce avoidable hospital readmission penalties by identifying preventable risk factors.

Clinical Insight: Uncover key clinical and demographic drivers behind frequent readmissions using diagnostic and exploratory analytics.

Key Analytics Deliverables
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Insights on readmission rates segmented by age, length of stay, discharge destination, and comorbidity count.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): Tracking overall 30-day readmission rate, average length of stay (LOS), emergency department (ED) visit frequency, and high-risk patient volume.

Statistical & Diagnostic Analysis: Hypothesis testing and correlation analysis identifying critical indicators (e.g., polypharmacy, prior inpatient visits).

Interactive BI Dashboard: Executive and operational views built in Power BI / Tableau / Excel for real-time monitoring and patient risk filtering.
